Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) is a proven approach for improving mental health by changing unhelpful thought patterns and behaviors. Here are five practical CBT tips you can start using today:

## 1. Identify Automatic Thoughts
Notice when you have a negative or unhelpful thought. Write it down. Awareness is the first step to change.

## 2. Challenge Unhelpful Beliefs
Ask yourself: Is this thought 100% true? What evidence supports or contradicts it? Try to see the situation from a different perspective.

## 3. Practice Thought Replacement
Replace negative thoughts with more balanced, realistic ones. For example, change “I always mess up” to “Sometimes I make mistakes, but I also succeed.”

## 4. Use Behavioral Experiments
Test your beliefs by trying new behaviors. If you think “If I ask for help, people will judge me,” try asking for help and observe what actually happens.

## 5. Schedule Pleasant Activities
Plan small enjoyable activities each day, even if you don’t feel like it. Positive experiences can improve your mood over time.
